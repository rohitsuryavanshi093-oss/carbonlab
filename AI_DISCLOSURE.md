AI Disclosure

CarbonLab is being developed with the help of AI tools.

I am using AI mainly because I am building this project alongside my exam preparation, and it allows me to move much faster with coding, debugging, documentation, and research organization.

How I use AI

AI tools may help me with:

- Writing and refactoring code
- Debugging errors
- Explaining programming concepts
- Suggesting software architecture
- Creating tests
- Organizing documentation
- Finding possible research directions
- Reviewing mathematical or computational approaches
- Improving the structure of research notes and reports

For some parts of the project, AI may generate a significant amount of the initial code. I review, test, modify, and integrate that work into the project.

What AI does not mean here

The use of AI does not mean that research results are being generated or accepted without checking.

CarbonLab is intended to be based on:

- real and traceable sources
- documented assumptions
- reproducible calculations
- explicit methodology
- testing
- uncertainty analysis
- honest reporting of limitations

AI-generated suggestions are treated as suggestions, not automatically as facts.

Important scientific claims, datasets, assumptions, calculations, and results should be checked against appropriate sources or computational tests before being used in the final research work.

My role

My role is to decide the overall direction of CarbonLab, its research questions, what I want the project to investigate, and what I consider useful or worth pursuing.

I am also responsible for reviewing the work and eventually learning the technical details behind the system.

Because I am currently prioritizing my exams, I am intentionally using AI as a development assistant rather than spending my preparation time manually writing every part of the code.

After my exams, I plan to go through the project properly and understand the architecture, mathematics, data, algorithms, experiments, and results in depth.

Research integrity

I will not use AI to fabricate:

- research results
- experimental results
- citations
- datasets
- sources
- competition participation
- academic credentials
- personal achievements

The earlier Carbon Removal XPRIZE-related activity mentioned in the project documentation was student brainstorming and was not an official competition entry.

I also will not present AI-generated work as if no AI tools were involved.

Why I am documenting this

I want the development process of CarbonLab to be transparent.

The goal is not to prove that every line of code was typed manually by me. The goal is to build something technically serious, reproducible, and scientifically useful, while being honest about how it was made.

This disclosure may be updated as the project develops and my use of AI changes.
